4. Ads and tracking

  • The ad system starts only after you, as an adult, agree on the screen shown when you first tap a video. If you say no, it does not start and the rest of the Game works.
  • Personalized ads are off by default. You can turn them on in Settings. On iPhone you will then also see Apple's tracking question. Your answer does not change your coins or the Game.
  • You can report an ad in Settings, and use the privacy icon inside the ad to reach Unity's choices.

5. Children and teenagers

  • Under 13: the Game runs in offline mode only. No account is created and we receive nothing. If we learn that we hold data of a child under 13, we delete it. Parents can contact us at the address above.
  • 13 to 17: no ads, no ad system, no advertising identifiers and no profiling. We do not show bonus or "best value" labels on coin packs.
  • We may use the age range your device or store shares with us, only to set the right experience.

6. Transfers to other countries

We are based in Belarus, our database is in Germany, and Unity and its partners may process data in the United States and other countries. These countries may not offer the same protection as yours. The risk is that you may have fewer ways to enforce your rights there, and authorities there may be able to access data. We transfer data to provide the service you asked for, under our providers' contract terms, and, for reward videos, with your consent.

7. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop processing it, and you can withdraw consent at any time without giving a reason. Withdrawing does not affect what was done before. Write to alexander.lyadkevich@gmail.com from your account's email. We answer requests for information within 5 working days and other requests within 15 days, and sooner where your law requires it.

You can delete your account in Settings at any time: see the deletion page. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country. In Belarus this is the National Personal Data Protection Center (cpd.by).

US residents: where your state's privacy law applies to us, you have the rights it gives you, including the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data and of targeted advertising. We do not discriminate against you for using them.

Unity decides how long it keeps ad data. To ask it to delete such data, contact DPO@unity3d.com.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections and store sign-in tokens in your device's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Changes

We publish changes here with a new version date. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the Game.
