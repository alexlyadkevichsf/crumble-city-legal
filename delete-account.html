Delete your Crumble City account

In the Game

  1. Open the Game and go to Settings (gear icon).
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

By email, if you cannot open the Game

Email alexander.lyadkevich@gmail.com from the address of your account, with the subject "Delete my Crumble City account". If you signed in with Apple and hid your email, tell us your nickname. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We delete it within 15 days.

What is deleted

Your sign-in details, nickname, progress, coins, items, achievements, match results and coin history, and your online player ID at Unity. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also revoke the Game's access to your Apple ID.

What is kept

Purchase records (transaction number, pack, date) for 7 years for accounting, no longer linked to you. Backups may hold deleted data for a short time until they are replaced. Unity may keep ad data under its own policy: contact DPO@unity3d.com.

Deletion cannot be undone, and purchased coins are lost. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.
