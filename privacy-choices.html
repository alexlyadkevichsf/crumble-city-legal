Your Privacy Choices

You control these in the Game under Settings → Legal & Privacy:

  • Reward videos. The ad system only runs if you are 18+ and agree. You can withdraw this at any time. It then stays off until you agree again.
  • Personalized ads. Off by default. When off, we tell ad partners that you opted out of the sale or sharing of your data.
  • iPhone tracking. Change it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID. You can delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

If you cannot open the Game, or you want to opt out of sale or sharing of personal data under a US state law, email alexander.lyadkevich@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy choices". You can also use the privacy icon inside an ad to reach Unity's own choices.
